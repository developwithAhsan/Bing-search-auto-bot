Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 8, 2026

1. Information Collection

Bing Rewards Auto Bot is committed to protecting your privacy. This tool operates locally in your browser and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to external servers.

2. Local Storage

The application uses browser localStorage to save:

  • Your Bing Rewards points count
  • Custom search interval settings
  • Search history and activity logs (stored locally only)

This data remains on your device and is never shared or uploaded.

3. Third-Party Services

This tool interfaces with:

  • Bing.com: All searches are performed through Bing's official search engine. Bing's own privacy policy applies to search activities.
  • Microsoft Rewards: Points earning is subject to Microsoft's Rewards terms and conditions.

4. Cookies

This application does not set its own cookies. However, Bing.com may set cookies as per their privacy policy when you use the embedded browser.

5. Data Security

All data processed by this tool remains within your browser. We do not have access to your Bing account credentials, search history, or personal information.

6. User Rights

You can clear all locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage or using your browser's privacy settings.

7. Changes to Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For privacy concerns or questions, please visit PostUpPK.com
